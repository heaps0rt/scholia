Privacy boundary

Scholia captures only after an explicit selection or capture gesture. The selected material, nearby context, and your question are sent to the provider you configure. There is no Scholia backend, analytics, or telemetry.

Because ordinary extension storage is not an operating-system keychain, use a dedicated, revocable provider key with a spending limit.
