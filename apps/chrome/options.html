Privacy boundary

Scholia captures only after an explicit selection or capture gesture. Short pages are included in full; long pages are packed locally into an outline and relevant excerpts before anything is sent to your provider. There is no Scholia backend, analytics, or telemetry.

Because ordinary extension storage is not an operating-system keychain, use a dedicated, revocable provider key with a spending limit.
